The Amazing Race

Scavenger Hunt & Problem Solving

The Amazing Race is an interactive category designed to test delegates’ thinking and detective skills through a simulated scavenger hunt.

Competition Overview

The Amazing Race has been designed as an entertaining and engaging part of Aitchison Business Concept. The category tests delegates’ thinking skills alongside their detective skills, requiring participants to solve problems and work through a scavenger-hunt-style experience.

The competition is simulated as a scavenger hunt, making the category an interactive experience rather than a conventional business presentation or written competition.
