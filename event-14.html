Corporate Canvas

Brand Identity & Brand Experience

Corporate Canvas challenges teams to build a coherent brand identity and translate that identity from digital marketing into a real-world brand experience.

Competition Rounds

Round 1 — Brand Identity & Visual Marketing

In the first round, each team is asked to completely revamp its company’s visual identity. Teams must select a new colour scheme consisting of exactly three colours that best represent their product, target market, and brand personality. Once the colour palette is finalised, teams will apply it consistently across a mock Instagram page created specifically for this round.

The page should include posts, highlights, and basic layout elements to show how the brand would look when marketed digitally. The focus of this round is on visual coherence, colour psychology, including an explanation of the HEX codes and why the colours relate to one another, creativity, and how effectively the brand communicates its product through social media.

Round 2 — Brand Experience & Stall Setup

Number of qualified teams: 15

Teams will bring their Round 1 brands to life by designing and setting up a stall for their company. The stall must use the same colour scheme as their Instagram page and reflect the brand’s personality. Teams will creatively display their products and engage visitors, demonstrating a clear understanding of their target audience and how the brand translates into a real-world experience.
