Finance & Accounting

Fiscal Frontier

Financial Analysis & Problem Solving

Fiscal Frontier tests delegates’ understanding of accounting and finance through timed questions, numerical reasoning, and practical financial decision-making.

Competition Rounds

Round 1 — Multiple Choice Challenge

In this round, delegates will answer 30 multiple-choice questions within a time limit of 45–50 minutes. The questions are designed to test understanding of financial concepts, accounting principles, and business decision-making under realistic scenarios. Topics include liquidity, profit versus cash, cost classification, receivables, payables, financing decisions, and financial ratios.

Delegates must apply numerical reasoning and business judgment to select the correct answers, considering both the quantitative and practical implications of each scenario.

Judging Criteria
Accuracy of answersSpeed of reasoningApplication of accounting and finance principles
Round 2 — Structured Problem Solving

Number of qualified teams: 20

This round challenges delegates with 20 structured questions requiring calculations, reasoning, and short analytical answers. Scenarios mirror real-world finance challenges such as working capital analysis, cost classification, cash flow planning, short-term financing decisions, and pricing strategies.

Delegates have 60–70 minutes to complete the round and must demonstrate practical application of accounting concepts, financial planning, and problem-solving skills. Solutions should be clear, logical, and show an understanding of how financial decisions affect both short-term stability and overall business health.

Judging Criteria
Accuracy of calculationsLogical financial reasoningPracticality of solutions
