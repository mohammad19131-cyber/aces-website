Chess

The Gentleman’s Game & Snakes and Ladders

Chess combines strategic thinking, foresight, and composure with a business-focused rapid-fire challenge that tests knowledge and decision-making under pressure.

Competition Rounds

Round 1 — The Gentleman’s Game

The first round will be a classic chess tournament designed to test strategic thinking, foresight, and composure under pressure. Teams will be divided into brackets and will compete in knockout-style matches. Each game will be played under a fixed time control to ensure efficiency and fairness.

The winner of each match will advance to the next stage, moving through the quarter-finals, semi-finals, and ultimately the final and determining match. In the case of a draw, tie-breakers such as blitz games or sudden-death moves may be used to determine a winner. This round rewards patience, planning, and the ability to anticipate an opponent’s moves, setting a strong intellectual foundation for the competition.

Round 2 — Snakes and Ladders

The qualified teams from the first round will proceed to a dynamic and interactive twist on the classic board game Snakes and Ladders. While progression on the board is determined by rolling a dice, each team must first earn its turn by participating in a rapid-fire question round.

These questions will be business-oriented, covering areas such as economics, management, entrepreneurship, and current affairs, and must be answered correctly within a strict time limit. Correct answers allow teams to roll the dice and advance, while incorrect responses result in a forfeited turn.

Snakes and Ladders introduces an element of unpredictability, ensuring that no position is entirely secure. This round blends luck with knowledge and quick decision-making, keeping participants engaged while rigorously testing their business acumen under time pressure.
