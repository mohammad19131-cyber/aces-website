Jurisprudential

Corporate Law, Analysis and Advocacy

This category tests delegates’ ability to understand legal issues in a corporate setting, propose practical legal corrections, and apply legal principles through structured argument and courtroom advocacy.

Competition Rounds

Round 1 — Contract Analysis & Rectification

In this round, delegates are given a short business scenario that involves a basic legal issue, such as a weak contract term, misleading advertising, data misuse, or breach of agreement. Teams must identify what the company did wrong, explain the legal and business risk created by the mistake, and propose a simple correction that protects the company while remaining commercially practical. The focus is on understanding how law affects business decisions and applying it effectively in a corporate setting.

Each team will have 3 minutes to present its legal correction, followed by 2 minutes of Q&A with the judges. This is an elimination round, and only teams that present clear, logical, and realistic corrections will qualify for Round 2.

Judging Criteria
Identification of legal mistakeExplanation of legal and business risksRealism of correctionStructure and clarity of presentation
Round 2 — Corporate Crime Moot Court

Number of qualified teams: 10

Round 2 simulates a formal law moot based on a corporate crime case study. Delegates will be provided with the case materials and a list of witnesses prior to the round. Each team will be assigned the role of either the prosecution or the defense. This round evaluates delegates’ advocacy skills, jurisprudential understanding, and ability to apply legal principles in an adversarial courtroom setting.

Each team will be given three minutes for opening statements, followed by three minutes for cross-examination of witnesses and two minutes for closing statements. Delegates are expected to present structured arguments, conduct effective questioning, and respond confidently under time constraints.

Teams will be evaluated on the clarity and strength of their legal arguments, application of relevant laws and jurisprudence, effectiveness of witness examination, courtroom conduct and time management, and the persuasiveness of their opening and closing statements. Round 2 determines the final standings and winners of the Jurisprudential category. Teams that demonstrate exceptional legal analysis, advocacy skills, and courtroom performance will be ranked highest.

Judging Criteria
Strength and clarity of legal argumentsEffectiveness of cross-questioningApplication of legal principlesPersuasiveness of opening and closing statementsTime management
