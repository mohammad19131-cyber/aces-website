Techeneurship

Startup Creation, Expansion & Crisis Management

Techeneurship challenges delegates to develop an innovative start-up, turn it into a viable business model, and defend its value under a high-pressure crisis.

Competition Rounds

Round 1 — Elevator Pitch

In this round, delegates will be required to deliver a concise and impactful elevator pitch within 90 seconds, presenting an original and innovative start-up idea for either a product or a service. The pitch must introduce the product and outline the business model, while providing key insights into the marketing strategy.

Delegates must clearly define the problem being addressed and the unique solution being proposed. Pitches should also demonstrate how the business will generate revenue and how marketing strategies will be implemented. The pitch will be followed by a Q&A session with the judges.

Only teams that demonstrate strong fundamentals, clear communication, and viable business concepts will advance to the next stage of the competition.

Judging Criteria
Structure and clarityFeasibility and viabilityInnovation and originalityQ&A session
Round 2 — Business Expansion & Strategic Evaluation

Number of qualified teams: 15

Round 2 builds upon the start-up ideas qualified from Round 1 and focuses on transforming a strong concept into a structured, feasible, and investment-ready business plan. This round evaluates the delegates’ ability to think analytically, plan strategically, and justify the long-term viability of their start-up.

Qualified teams will deliver a 6–8 minute formal presentation that expands on their original elevator pitch, demonstrating depth of research, financial understanding, and strategic clarity. Following the presentation, teams will participate in a Q&A session with the judges for 2–3 minutes.

Judging Criteria
Depth of research and market understandingFinancial viabilityStrategic clarityQuality of presentationQ&A session
Round 3 — Crisis: Sell Your Business

Round 3 is the final and most intensive stage of the competition. Teams entering this round are assumed to have developed a structured and feasible business model in Round 2. Each team will be presented with a major crisis scenario designed by the judges. The crisis may involve situations such as imminent bankruptcy due to cash-flow shortages, reputational or public-relations damage, regulatory or legal challenges, market disruption, or competitive threats.

The primary objective is to convince the panel of judges to invest in the company and save it from bankruptcy. Teams must demonstrate that, despite the crisis, the business remains salvageable, scalable, and worthy of investment, while displaying expert negotiation skills and communicating effectively.

Teams will be given 10 minutes of preparation time to analyze the crisis and structure their strategy. Delegates will then have 2 minutes to present their risk-mitigation strategies and 3 minutes to negotiate with the judges to secure investment. Following the response, judges will engage in an intensive Q&A session lasting 2 minutes.

Judging Criteria
Clarity and logicRealism and effectivenessPersuasion, composure and critical thinkingInvestment from judges
