Social Entrepreneurship

Business Solutions with Social Value

Social Entrepreneurship challenges delegates to analyze company problems, develop practical solutions, and consider the wider effects of business decisions on stakeholders and communities.

Competition Rounds

Round 1 — Company Portfolio Presentation

In this round, delegates are given a company portfolio containing details about the company’s history, ownership, structure, and recent developments or crises. They must carefully analyze the portfolio, identify the core problem, and prepare a presentation proposing practical, implementable solutions.

Judges evaluate delegates based on critical analysis, creativity, feasibility, and presentation skills. Delegates should focus on understanding the company fully, analyzing the root causes of the problem, developing short-term and long-term solutions, and considering the impact on stakeholders such as customers, employees, and the community.

Presentations should be structured with a brief introduction, problem analysis, proposed solutions with justifications, and a persuasive conclusion.

Judging Criteria
Critical analysisOriginality of ideasFeasibility of solutionsPresentation skills
Round 2 — Press Conference

Number of qualified teams: 15

In this round, delegates will assume that the initiatives proposed in Round 1 have placed the company under public and media attention. Judges will assume the role of news reporters, conducting a press conference to assess the company’s social responsibility, credibility, and impact.

Delegates will be questioned on the company’s objectives, decision-making, ethical standards, community engagement, and overall social value. Teams are expected to respond clearly and confidently, demonstrating transparency, accountability, and sensitivity towards stakeholder and community concerns.
