Investomania

Interactive Stock Market Simulation

Investomania is an interactive stock market simulation in which teams manage an allocated budget, respond to market updates, and make strategic investment decisions across a fictional portfolio of companies.

Competition Overview

Investomania is an interactive stock market simulation spanning over two rounds. Delegates, armed with an allocated budget, will navigate a dynamic portfolio of companies, strategizing to buy and sell shares as market updates roll in throughout the event. The category prioritizes quick thinking and a deep understanding of how the stock market operates, as delegates invest in different companies based on scenarios detailed in the updates. The team with the highest profit claims victory.

Round Guidelines

Prior to the first round, delegates will be introduced to the fictional stock market through several detailed company portfolios. They will be allocated a certain budget once the round begins, which they must invest in the different companies provided in the portfolios based on the given crisis updates.

Since delegates will buy and sell shares through an online platform, they must bring their own device, with laptops recommended, as well as a stable Internet connection.

Investomania is not an elimination category, and all trades made in the first round will carry over into the second round. At the end of the second round, all assets will be liquidated, and the team with the most capital will be declared the winner.
