MUNey

CEO Diplomacy, Directives & Crisis Simulation

MUNey reimagines Model United Nations by placing delegates in the roles of global CEOs, combining traditional MUN procedure with corporate strategy, alliances, and crisis management.

Competition Overview

This category redefines the traditional Model United Nations (MUN). Instead of representing countries, delegates take on the roles of renowned CEOs from leading global corporations.

To guide participants, a comprehensive study guide will be provided in advance. This guide offers detailed insights into the global challenge that will serve as the central focus of the conference. It includes background information, industry-specific perspectives, and an overview of each CEO’s company and strategic interests, ensuring that delegates are well-prepared to navigate the committee’s complex discussions.

Delegates will collaborate to develop a public directive that proposes solutions to the shared challenge. The format also allows for private directives, enabling strategic decision-making, alliances, and maneuvering.

Throughout the day, a crisis simulation will unfold, introducing real-time challenges that test participants’ adaptability and leadership skills, mirroring the high-pressure situations faced by global CEOs.

This MUNey committee offers an unparalleled blend of traditional MUN principles with the realism of corporate leadership. It will follow the rules and regulations as well as the judging criteria of a regular MUN.
