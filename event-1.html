Build Your Own Idea

Product Creation & Business Thinking

Build Your Own Idea combines creative product development with business knowledge, testing resource management, prototyping, quick thinking, market understanding, and logic.

Competition Rounds

Round 1 — Build Your Own Product: Idea Auction and Prototype Creation

In this round, delegates participate in the foundational and elimination stage of the competition. Teams are required to strategically acquire components through a live auction and then use those items to design and construct a physical, handmade product. Delegates must manage limited capital, think creatively under pressure, and convert abstract ideas into a tangible prototype.

Each team takes part in a live auction where components and functional elements are bid on using assigned cards that are scanned to exchange money. After the auction, teams are given a fixed amount of time to build a working prototype using only the items they have purchased along with a basic set of standardized DIY materials. Judges assess how effectively teams allocate resources, collaborate, and produce a viable product within strict constraints.

At the end of the build phase, teams present and explain their product, covering its concept, functionality, resource management, and creative aspects, followed by a short Q&A session where judges evaluate decision-making, justification of trade-offs, and critical thinking under pressure.

Judging Criteria
Clarity and coherence of product concept Integration of auctioned components Functionality and practicality of prototype Resource allocation Q&A session
Round 2 — Family Feud

Number of qualified teams: 16

Round 2 is a Family Feud-style game that tests teams on quick thinking, market understanding, and logic. Teams must give the most popular and practical answers, not just technically correct ones. The round will be played in a tournament (knockout) format: teams will compete one-on-one against another team in each match. The winning team progresses to the next stage, while the losing team is eliminated.

In the initial matches, each team will get one guess per question. The team with the better or higher-ranked answer moves forward. As teams advance, the number of guesses per team increases, making later rounds more challenging and strategic. Questions will be related to all things business and entrepreneurship.

Teams score points based on how popular their answers are. The team with the higher total score in each matchup advances. The final winning team is declared the overall winner of the competition. Teams must answer quickly and clearly. No external help or discussion during guesses unless allowed. Judges’ decisions and answer rankings are final.
